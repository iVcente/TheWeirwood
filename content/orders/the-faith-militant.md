The armed wing of [[The Faith of the Seven|the Faith of the Seven]], raised in two orders of very different character. The Warrior's Sons were knights of birth and means who set aside lands and titles to serve the Seven; the Poor Fellows were the other end of the same impulse — unarmoured, unlettered, and numerous, carrying axes and painted stars rather than steel.

Both took up arms against [[House Targaryen]] in [[The Faith Militant Uprising]], and both were broken by [[Maegor I Targaryen|Maegor the Cruel]], who put a bounty on their heads and rode [[Balerion]] against them. Jaehaerys I ended the matter by treaty rather than fire: the orders disarmed, and the Crown guaranteed the Faith its freedom of worship.
