The dominant religion of the Seven Kingdoms south of the Neck, brought to Westeros by the Andals and holding that one god shows seven faces — Father, Mother, Warrior, Smith, Maiden, Crone and Stranger. Its quarrel with [[House Targaryen]] was doctrinal before it was ever military: the Faith condemned the Targaryen practice of marrying sibling to sibling, and of taking more than one wife.

That quarrel armed itself in [[The Faith Militant|the militant orders]] — the Warrior's Sons and the Poor Fellows — and became [[The Faith Militant Uprising]] under Aenys I and [[Maegor I Targaryen|Maegor the Cruel]]. Jaehaerys I ended it by negotiation rather than dragonfire, trading the disarmament of the orders for the Crown's guarantee of the Faith's freedom of worship.
