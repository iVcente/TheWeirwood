A record of what this site is built from, and of the work borrowed from other hands to build it.

## The artwork

Every mark on this site comes from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/), used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). All of them have been altered: stripped of the black ground they are published on, and recoloured to the palette of these pages.

The heart tree -- standing behind the wordmark on the front page, and in the card this site shows when a link to it is shared -- is the **[Dead wood icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/dead-wood.html)** by [Lorc](https://lorcblog.blogspot.com/). It has been re-cropped as well, so the crown clears the top of the page and the roots come to rest on the section boxes.

The carved face -- watching from the browser tab, and standing at the left of the bar on every page -- is the **[Tree face icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/cathelineau/tree-face.html)** by Cathelineau.

The glyph on the greensight -- the door to the graph, on every page and at the foot of every entry -- is the **[Mesh network icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/mesh-network.html)** by [Delapouite](https://delapouite.com/).

The mark on a tag -- in the bar of a tag page, and standing behind its title -- is the **[Grouped drops icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/grouped-drops.html)** by Lorc. One falling apart into many is what a tag is: a cut across the sections rather than a place inside one.

## The site

Built on [Quartz v5](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) by Jacky Zhao and contributors, released under the MIT licence.

Set in Cinzel for headings, EB Garamond for prose, and IBM Plex Mono for the ledger voice -- the metadata boxes, the breadcrumbs, and the credit bar at the foot of every page. All three are open licensed and served by Google Fonts.

## The histories

The histories themselves are from the one and only George R. R. Martin. The articles here are my own summaries and commentary. Where passages are quoted directly, they're attributed to its book and chapter.
