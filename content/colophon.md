A record of what this site is built from, and of the work borrowed from other hands to build it.

## The artwork

Two icons from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/), both used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), and both altered for this site.

The heart tree -- standing behind the wordmark on the front page, and in the card this site shows when a link to it is shared -- is the **[Dead wood icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/dead-wood.html)** by [Lorc](https://lorcblog.blogspot.com/). It has been recoloured from white to the site's red, stripped of its black ground, and re-cropped so the crown clears the top of the page and the roots come to rest on the section boxes.

The carved face watching from the browser tab is the **[Tree face icon](https://game-icons.net/1x1/cathelineau/tree-face.html)** by Cathelineau. It has been recoloured to the same red and set on the site's own dark ground.

## The site

Built on [Quartz v5](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) by Jacky Zhao and contributors, released under the MIT licence.

Set in Cinzel for headings, EB Garamond for prose, and IBM Plex Mono for the ledger voice -- the metadata boxes, the breadcrumbs, and the credit bar at the foot of every page. All three are open licensed and served by Google Fonts.

## The histories

The histories themselves are from the one and only George R. R. Martin. The articles here are my own summaries and commentary. Where passages are quoted directly, they're attributed to its book and chapter.
