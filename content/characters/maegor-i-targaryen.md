The third king of the [[House Targaryen|Targaryen]] dynasty. Second son of Aegon the Conqueror by his sister-wife Visenya, Maegor seized the Iron Throne in 42 AC from his nephew Aenys I's line and reigned until 48 AC, riding [[Balerion]] the Black Dread into every battle. His brutal suppression of the [[The Faith Militant Uprising|Faith Militant Uprising]] and his six marriages earned him the epithet *the Cruel*. He was born at [[Dragonstone]] and died on the Iron Throne under circumstances that remain contested; his nephew Jaehaerys I succeeded him.
