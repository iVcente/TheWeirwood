A Valyrian dragonlord family that fled [[Valyria|the Freehold]] twelve years before the Doom and settled on [[Dragonstone]]. Under Aegon the Conqueror they subdued six of the Seven Kingdoms and ruled from [[The Iron Throne|the Iron Throne]] for nearly three centuries. Their bond with dragons — including [[Balerion]] the Black Dread, mount to three kings — was both the source of their supremacy and, after the [[Dance of the Dragons]], the beginning of their decline. Among their early members, [[Maegor I Targaryen|Maegor the Cruel]] stands as the reign that nearly ended the dynasty in its first generation.
