An ancient Valyrian fortress on an island of the same name in Blackwater Bay, and the ancestral seat of [[House Targaryen]] in Westeros since they fled [[Valyria|the Freehold]] before the Doom. Its walls, shaped into the likenesses of dragons by lost Valyrian arts, sheltered [[Balerion]] and generations of dragonriders. [[Maegor I Targaryen|Maegor the Cruel]] was born within its halls. By tradition, the heir apparent to [[The Iron Throne|the Iron Throne]] holds Dragonstone as their seat.
