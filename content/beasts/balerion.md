The largest dragon known to have lived in Westeros, and the last dragon born in [[Valyria|Old Valyria]] before the Doom. Brought west by [[House Targaryen]] when they fled to [[Dragonstone]] a dozen years before the Freehold fell, he was ridden by Aegon the Conqueror during [[Aegon's Conquest|the Conquest]] — his flames forged [[The Iron Throne|the Iron Throne]] itself. He later carried [[Maegor I Targaryen|Maegor the Cruel]] through [[The Faith Militant Uprising|the Faith Militant Uprising]], and in his final years was mount to Viserys I. He died of old age in 94 AC.
